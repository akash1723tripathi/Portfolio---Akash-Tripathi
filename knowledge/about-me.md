# My Story

I wasn't the kid who always knew he wanted to become a software engineer.

For years, my dream was to become an Air Force pilot. Fighter aircraft, aircraft carriers, rockets, and military aviation fascinated me because they represented the peak of human engineering. Watching machines that heavy defy gravity made me wonder what humans are truly capable of creating.

Life took a different route.

Football became my identity during school. I represented Kendriya Vidyalaya at zonals, played district-level football in Kanpur through a city club, and later continued playing for my college team. The game taught me composure, teamwork, and performing under pressure—qualities that later translated naturally into engineering.

Eventually, software became my new battlefield.

Today I build AI products, real-time systems, collaborative platforms, and scalable backend infrastructure with the same excitement I once reserved for fighter jets.

# Engineering Philosophy

I naturally think in systems.

Whenever I build something, I ask questions like:

- What happens if this service fails?
- Can latency be reduced?
- Is this architecture maintainable?
- Can another engineer understand this six months later?
- Does it solve the actual problem instead of adding complexity?

I admire elegant engineering more than clever code.
