## Engineering Philosophy

When working with clients, Akash follows one principle:

> Build software that remains maintainable after the invoice is paid.

He prioritizes:

- clean architecture,
- type safety,
- scalability,
- documentation,
- and measurable performance improvements.

The objective isn't shipping fast—it is shipping something the client can confidently grow.
